# REVEREND INSANITY: WORLD LORE & GAME DESIGN BIBLE

## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY
The fundamental rule of this world is: **"Man is the spirit of all living beings, Gu are the essence of heaven and earth"**. 
- **Players/Humans:** Have no inherent physical strength or magical spells. They act solely as vessels (Apertures) storing Primeval Essence (Mana).
- **Gu Bugs:** The source of all abilities, weapons, defenses, and utility.

## 2. GU SYSTEM & CLASSIFICATIONS
Gu bugs are graded from **Rank 1 to Rank 9**. Ranks 1-5 are Mortal Gu; Ranks 6-9 are Immortal Gu (unique, only one exists in the world at a time).

**Functional Categories:**
- **Attack (Công kích):** Deals damage (e.g., Moonlight Gu, Bone Spear Gu).
- **Defense (Phòng ngự):** Shields or bodily modifications (e.g., Jade Skin Gu).
- **Healing/Support (Trị liệu/Phụ trợ):** Heals wounds or refines essence (e.g., Liquor Worm, Self-Reliance Gu).
- **Movement (Di động):** Speed and evasion (e.g., Fixed Immortal Travel Gu).
- **Investigation (Trinh sát):** Scouting and gathering intel.
- **Storage (Tồn trữ):** Inventory management (e.g., Tusita Flower).

**Advanced Combat Concepts:**
- **Killer Moves (Sát chiêu):** Combining multiple Gu bugs to unleash a synergistic and powerful effect.
- **Immortal Gu Houses (Tiên Cổ Phòng):** The pinnacle of formation path; mobile war fortresses integrating offense, defense, and movement.

## 3. GEOGRAPHY (MAP DESIGN)
The world is vast, divided into **Five Regions and Two Heavens**, separated by massive "Regional Walls" that suppress outsiders.

**The Five Regions:**
1. **Southern Border (Nam Cương):** Defined by endless mountains (e.g., Qing Mao Mountain). Ruled by clan-based societies.
2. **Northern Plains (Bắc Nguyên):** Vast grasslands plagued by decennial blizzards. Highly aggressive, nomadic tribes. Home to the Imperial Court Blessed Land.
3. **Central Continent (Trung Châu):** The most resource-rich region. Ruled by the Sect system (Ten Great Ancient Sects) and the supreme Heavenly Court.
4. **Eastern Sea (Đông Hải):** Endless oceans and archipelagos. The wealthiest region. Features dangerous zones like the Turbulent Flow Sea Area.
5. **Western Desert (Tây Mạc):** Boundless deserts and oases. Society relies heavily on merchant caravans.

**The Two Heavens:**
Above the regions are the **White Heaven** and **Black Heaven**, home to Immemorial Desolate Beasts and hidden forces.

**Secluded Domains of Heaven and Earth:**
Unique map nodes containing supreme Dao Marks (e.g., Dang Hun Mountain, Luo Po Valley, Reverse Flow River).

## 4. CULTIVATION PATHS (CLASSES/MASTERY)
History is shaped by the creation of new Paths. Mastery levels range from Ordinary -> Master -> Grandmaster -> Supreme Grandmaster.
- **Time Path (Trụ Đạo) & Space Path (Vũ Đạo):** Manipulating the River of Time and spatial dimensions.
- **Strength Path (Lực Đạo):** Relies on physical beast phantoms.
- **Wisdom Path (Trí Đạo):** Masters of deduction, plotting, and mind reading. Formulated by Thoughts, Wills, and Emotions.
- **Enslavement Path (Nô Đạo):** Controlling beast armies to fight one against many.
- **Dream Path (Mộng Đạo):** The path of the future era. Exploring dream realms to directly steal true meaning and raise mastery levels.
- *Other Paths:* Fire, Water, Earth, Sword, Refinement, Soul, Blood, Luck, etc.

## 5. ECOSYSTEM & RACES
- **Humans:** The current dominant race.
- **Variant Humans (Dị Nhân):** Ten races pushed to the brink: 
  - **Hairy Men (Người Lông):** Born with innate talent for Gu Refinement.
  - **Rockmen (Người Đá):** Sturdy, sleep to nourish their souls, experts at underground mining.
  - **Snowmen, Inkmen, Mermen, Feathermen**, etc.
- **Beast & Plant Tiers:** Wild Beasts -> Mutated Beasts -> Desolate Beasts (Rank 6 equivalent) -> Ancient Desolate -> Immemorial Desolate.

## 6. RESOURCES & ECONOMY
- **Currencies:** **Primeval Stones** for Mortals (Ranks 1-5). **Immortal Essence Stones** for Gu Immortals (Ranks 6-9).
- **Gu Feeding:** "Raising a Gu is like keeping a mistress". Gu bugs will die if not fed their specific, often rare, food. This is the ultimate resource sink forcing players to explore and trade.
- **Aperture Management:** At endgame, Immortals must manage their own inner pocket world (Blessed Lands), managing weather, breeding beasts, and planting resources to sustain themselves.

## 7. AI AGENT GAME DESIGN GUIDELINES
- **Theme:** The game has no traditional "heroes." Every NPC and player action is driven by **BENEFITS** and **SURVIVAL**.
- **Mechanics:** Combat is a calculated exchange of Mana (Primeval Essence), Dao Mark multipliers, and Gu counters.
- **Economy:** Players must always feel the pressure of maintaining their Gu bugs (Hunger system) and acquiring Primeval Stones.
